October to December 2026, locking in and learning frontier AI, hands-on.

I'm building a home assistant that takes on the mental load of running a home. I'm writing the whole agent framework by hand, so I can understand what breaks: memory, monitoring, guardrails, skills, permissions and the rest. The basic shape is in place: [pyagi](https://github.com/TTitcombe/pyagi).
