---
title: Practical Defences Against Model Inversion Attacks for Split Neural Networks
authors: T. Titcombe, A. J. Hall, P. Papadopoulos, D. Romanini
year: 2021
venue: ICLR Workshop on Distributed and Private Machine Learning
arxiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05743
---

Split neural networks used for federated learning can leak private data to a malicious server through model inversion. Adding noise defends against it at an acceptable cost to accuracy, and combined with NoPeekNN it's needed to protect the data fully.
