Haidt lays out a convincing claim that kids (and adults) have been seriously harmed over the last two decades, and places the blame on the 1-2 hit of social media, and the closing off of free, outside play. The book is something most people believe _must_ be true in a loose sense, but he weaves a convincing argument by collecting various studies. Must-read territory for most of us.
